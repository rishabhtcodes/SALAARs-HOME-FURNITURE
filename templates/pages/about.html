Our Story · {{ SITE.name }}

Born from a Passion for Solid Wood

SALAAR's HOME was established with one timeless belief: furniture should be honest, beautiful, and made to outlast trends. In a world full of disposable board pieces, we chose to stand by authentic solid timber and handcrafted joinery.

From complete bedroom suites to made-to-measure wardrobes and delicate bedside tables, every piece that leaves our Karachi workshop is seasoned, shaped, and polished by masters of the craft.
